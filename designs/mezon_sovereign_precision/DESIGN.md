## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-precision, authoritative digital workspace engineered for elite advocates, corporate counsels, and judiciary researchers navigating the legal landscape of Uzbekistan. 

The aesthetic is a calibrated intersection of Silicon Valley engineering rigor and judicial gravity. It avoids generic corporate SaaS tropes, favoring the tactile feeling of a dark, obsidian-clad mission control console. Dense data structures, citation-level typography, and instant visual validation create a zero-latency, high-conviction environment.

- **Atmosphere:** Sovereign, focused, nocturnal, and uncompromisingly precise.
- **Emotional Response:** Certainty, intellectual command, impenetrable security, and surgical clarity.
- **Visual Stance:** Precision Dark Mode. Tonal layer separation through deep dark slates, reinforced with subtle structural borders (hairline 1px dividers), micro-glow indicators for AI verifications, and monospaced judicial anchors for statutory citations (`lex.uz`).

## Colors

The palette operates in strict adherence to dark-canvas luminance ratios, guaranteeing WCAG AAA legibility for extended nocturnal discovery sessions and contract audits.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Obsidian Base (`#0B0F17`):** The master canvas, anchoring deep focus and zero-bleed contrast.
- **Slate Layer 1 (`#121824`):** Primary paneling, persistent navigation bars, and inspection drawers.
- **Slate Layer 2 (`#1A2234`):** Elevated cards, context menus, hover states, and interactive input wells.
- **Subtle Surface Border (`#242F48`):** A hairline boundary ensuring visual containment without excessive luminance.

### Strategic Accents
- **Electric Legal Blue (`#3B82F6`):** Primary action token, indicating algorithmic synthesis, primary queries, and real-time state changes.
- **Judicial Gold (`#E5C158`):** Secondary accent reserved strictly for authoritative statutory anchors, Supreme Court precedents, and premium sovereign tier verifications.
- **Emerald Verification Green (`#10B981`):** Tertiary accent representing validated clauses, uncompromised audit checks, and active legal compliance.
- **Contradiction Crimson (`#EF4444`):** Error and alert accent signaling statutory friction, invalid citations, and breach points.
- **Warning Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Precautionary accent denoting pending amendments, ambiguous precedent, and drafting risks.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy accommodates high-volume bilingual legal drafting in Uzbek (Latin and Cyrillic) and Russian, while maintaining micro-density integrity.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans:** Selected for primary narrative structures, headings, dialogs, and contract body copy. Its structural clarity avoids the sterile monotony of generic grotesque fonts while maximizing optical readability against high-contrast dark backdrops.
- **JetBrains Mono:** The non-negotiable standard for code-level legal data. Every reference to `lex.uz`, presidential decrees (PF-series), resolution numbers, timestamps, SHA-256 validation signatures, and cross-statutory citations must be rendered exclusively in JetBrains Mono.
- **Weight Strategy:** Headlines favor semi-bold and bold weights with negative letter-spacing for razor-sharp authority. Micro-labels, citations, and status metadata use medium and semi-bold monospace variants for maximum legibility at tiny scale.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a high-density, 12-column fluid grid system engineered for multi-pane split workflows (e.g., source document alongside real-time AI legal analysis and citation graph).

### Spatial Hierarchy
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 12-column dynamic framework with `gutter-desktop` (1.5rem / 24px) and `margin-desktop` (2rem / 32px). Allows tripartite division: Navigation Rails (80px or 260px expanded), Active Document Viewer (6 columns), and Mezon Intelligence Synthesis Pane (4 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with 1rem gutters and 1.5rem margins. Collapses the intelligence panel into a slide-over drawer or toggleable split screen.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 1rem margins and gutters. Interfaces shift from parallel inspection to tabbed sequential scrutiny.

### Density Rules
Padding within elements adheres to tight 4px baseline intervals (`space-xs` to `space-xl`). Because legal professionals process large volumes of information per screen, excessive blank space is avoided in favor of deliberate, compact information architecture.

## Elevation & Depth

To sustain deep black fidelity and avoid muddy displays, elevation is expressed strictly through luminance stacking (tonal layering) paired with structural ghost borders and focused micro-tinted halos. Heavy, unfocused drop shadows are prohibited.

### Tonal Stratification
- **Ground Floor (Z0):** Base canvas (`#0B0F17`). The primary structural ground for document drafting and data streaming.
- **Tier 1 Surface (Z1):** Working panels, sidebars, header rails (`#121824`), bounded by a 1px solid hairline border in `#242F48`.
- **Tier 2 Surface (Z2):** Floating cards, active document review segments, contextual insight widgets (`#1A2234`).
- **Tier 3 Overlays (Z3):** Citation tooltips, modal sheets, and quick-search command palettes (`#1A2234`), highlighted with a subtle top edge stroke (`#3B82F6` at 30% opacity) and an ambient dark halo: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.

### Laser-Accented Edges
Interactive active states use directional ambient glow rather than full drop-shadows. A selected clause or citation emits an electric blue or judicial gold micro-feathered contour (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #3B82F6, 0 0 16px -2px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The design system enforces architectural sharpness with `roundedness: 1` (Soft), utilizing 4px (`0.25rem`) corner radiuses as the foundational standard.

- **Micro Components (Buttons, Citations, Input Bars):** 4px (`0.25rem`) standard radius. Communicates surgical software discipline without brutalist harshness.
- **Card Containers & Modules (`rounded-lg`):** 8px (`0.5rem`) radius. Maintains structural discipline across complex nested panels.
- **Command Palettes & Sovereign Dialogs (`rounded-xl`):** 12px (`0.75rem`) radius. Used exclusively for top-level floating modals and sovereign legal advisories.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Rounded-full styling is reserved exclusively for system status pips, avatar badges, and binary system verification indicators.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Sovereign Action:** Electric Blue background (`#3B82F6`), high-contrast dark text (`#0B0F17`) or pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with 600 weight, 4px corner radius. On hover: slight brightness elevate with a concentrated 12px blue field glow.
- **Judicial Secondary Button:** Slate Layer 2 (`#1A2234`) with a 1px border (`#242F48`), text rendered in Judicial Gold (`#E5C158`). Hover transitions border color to `#E5C158` at 60% opacity.
- **Ghost/Destructive Actions:** No background fill; hairline red border (`#EF4444`) with Crimson typography for irreversible redacting or termination clauses.

### Citation Chips & Statutory Badges
- **Statutory Link (`lex.uz` / Code Article):** JetBrains Mono 11px uppercase label, enclosed in `#121824` with a 1px border of `#242F48`. Prefixed by a 6px Judicial Gold orb. Interacting reveals a pinned popover showing the authentic law text.
- **Verification Pills:** Emerald Green surface (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) bounded by `#10B981` at 40% opacity, featuring white/emerald JetBrains Mono text.

### Form Inputs & Query Terminals
- Deep inset background (`#0B0F17`) framed by 1px slate boundaries (`#242F48`). 
- Focus state instantly trades the boundary for Electric Legal Blue (`#3B82F6`) with an inner 1px shadow, accompanied by a monospace counter indicator (`JetBrains Mono`) for tokens or parameter filters.

### Legal Data Cards & Contradiction Alerts
- **Clause Audit Card:** Background `#121824`, left border inset 3px solid:
  - Electric Blue for analytical summaries.
  - Emerald Green for fully validated clauses.
  - Contradiction Crimson for high-risk liability exposures.
- Typography within cards separates statutory commentary (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, 14px) from cited code sections (`JetBrains Mono`, 12px, indented with a muted left border).

### Document Comparison & Redline Lists
- Split rows with absolute baseline grid matching. Removed provisions receive Crimson micro-strikethroughs with faint crimson ground tint (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.08)`). Inserted legislation uses Emerald accents (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`).
